## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal trading observation desk keeps the current mandate, evidence, orders and money visible together. It uses useful tables and an adjacent decision log, with no marketing hero or decorative gauges.

OWN-WORLD: Cool off-white working paper, deep blue-teal navigation, restrained teal active controls and amber uncertainty. System/PingFang UI typography, tabular numerals, fine separators and compact inputs; generous whitespace separates tasks, not every record.

STORY: Appoint an Agent, inspect real market candidates, set a bounded paper mandate, run it, and follow concrete order/position records. The simulation banner remains visible on every page; live readiness is explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A narrow left navigation rail, compact title and mode row, full-width mandate strip, then a wide market table with a narrower selected-market/Agent detail panel. Scan and stop stay in the top action area. The signature interaction is selecting a market row to align its price, order book and review without losing the table.

FORM: Scientific observation log, candidate 3, seed 3f03f667. This surface is a desktop trading tool with a single-column fallback at narrow native window sizes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
